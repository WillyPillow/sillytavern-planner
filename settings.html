Planner → Writer
Before each reply, the planner model drafts what should happen next. The plan is injected into the prompt, and your main connection (the writer) writes the reply.

Planner

Profiles come from the Connection Profiles panel (API, model, preset, and instruct template for text completion). Leave unselected to plan with the current connection, i.e. the writer plans for itself.
The Connection Manager extension is disabled or unavailable. Enable it to pick a planner profile.
